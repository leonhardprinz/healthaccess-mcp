About

A free, open source alternative to Health Auto Export

If you want an AI to read your Apple Health and Apple Watch data, Health Access MCP does it for free. The iPhone app writes your workouts and daily health data as JSON into a folder you choose, automatically after every workout, and a local MCP server lets Claude, Cursor or another AI read it. The source is open under the MIT licence. Health Auto Export is the broader export tool and the better choice for some things, and this page says which.

When Health Auto Export is the better choice

When you want everything Apple Health holds in a spreadsheet, a dashboard or a home-automation system. It handles more metrics and formats and sends them to more places. It's also been on the App Store since 2016. Health Access MCP leaves all of that out on purpose.

When Health Access MCP is the better choice

When it's an AI that reads your data. The files have what an AI needs to coach you: every interval of a workout with its name, every pool length with stroke and SWOLF, heart rate every few seconds, and sleep, HRV and resting heart rate per day. The automatic export is free and the code is open. Of the two apps, it's the only one that sends a workout back to your Watch.

Set it up

  1. Install Health Access on the App Store, pick a folder that syncs to your computer and allow Health access.
  2. Give your AI the server: npx -y healthaccess-mcp. Setup per client: Claude Code, Claude Desktop, Cursor, ChatGPT.
  3. Ask it about your last workout.

Other free options

Apple's own export is free but manual, and you get one large XML file each time. Health Bridge for AI is also free and open source. It's read-only and uses a receiver you run yourself. All of them in one table.

The facts are from each product's App Store listing or website, read on 25 Oct and 16 Nov 2026. Prices change and differ by country. Health Auto Export is made by its own developer and isn't affiliated with this project. If something is wrong, write to support@healthaccessmcp.com.
