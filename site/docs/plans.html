Plans and "Put on Watch"

Your AI writes one small JSON file. The app turns it into a custom workout with the step names on your wrist and puts it on the Watch, one tap. When you swim it, the exported session comes back with those names on every interval. This page is the format, the rules and the flow.

The flow

  1. Your AI calls push_plan with the plan below (or writes the file itself, or you run healthaccess plan push session.json). The server validates it, fills id, created_at and schema, and writes plans/<id>.json. The id is the UTC time plus the slug of the name: 2026-05-13T19:02:11Z-s12.
  2. The folder syncs to the phone. The app scans plans/ on launch, when it comes to the foreground and after every export, so the plan shows up in the app's Import tab as "Your AI wrote S27", with the day and time it will go onto the Watch. If the app was not in front, you get a notification.
  3. It goes onto the Watch. WorkoutKit schedules it in the Watch's Workout app, under Scheduled, for the plan's scheduled_for; a plan without a time (or whose time has passed) gets the next morning at 10:00. The app checks the scheduler afterwards, so the status is a fact. Two ways:
    • One tap (the default): "Put on Watch" on the plan, then a confirmation that names the day and time.
    • Automatically: with "Put new plans on my Watch automatically" switched on in the Import tab, the app does it on its own the next time it runs or iOS wakes it, usually within a few hours, and sends a notification per plan. iOS does not wake apps the second a file lands in iCloud Drive, so this is not instant.
    "Remove from Watch" takes a scheduled plan off again and marks it cancelled. "Preview on Watch" under See parts opens Apple's sheet instead, which installs a plain custom workout without a date and does not report back (method: preview). Apple Fitness+ programs stay Apple's; plans appear in the Workout app.
  4. The app writes plans/<id>.status.json: scheduled, failed (with the error text) or cancelled. Your AI reads it with get_plan_status; until the file exists the state is pending.
  5. You do the session. The Watch shows each step's label. HealthKit drops the labels when it saves the workout, but it keeps the WorkoutKit id, so the exported file gets an exact plan_ref and every step gets its label back from the plan.

The format

{
  "name": "S27",                         # shown in the app and on the Watch, 40 characters at most
  "sport": "pool_swim",                  # run, cycle, walk, pool_swim, indoor_row, ... (list below)
  "pool_length_m": 50,                   # a reminder for you; the Watch keeps its own setting
  "warmup":   {"goal": {"type": "time_s", "value": 120}, "zone": 1},
  "blocks": [
    {"repeat": 4, "steps": [
      {"kind": "work",     "goal": {"type": "distance_m", "value": 50}, "zone": 1, "label": "FR easy, long strokes"},
      {"kind": "recovery", "goal": {"type": "time_s", "value": 30}}
    ]}
  ],
  "cooldown": {"goal": {"type": "distance_m", "value": 100}, "zone": 1},
  "notes": "what this session is for, shown in the app",
  "scheduled_for": "2026-10-27T07:30:00Z" # optional; also used to match the finished workout
}

Rules the server checks

  • sport is one of run, indoor_run, walk, hike, cycle, indoor_cycle, pool_swim, open_water_swim, indoor_row, hiit or elliptical. hiit and elliptical take time goals only.
  • name is not empty and at most 40 characters.
  • At least one block. repeat from 1 to 99, each block with at least one step.
  • goal.type is time_s or distance_m, with a positive value.
  • zone, when given, is 1 to 5. These are the Watch's own heart-rate zones; the Watch alerts when you leave the zone.
  • label at most 40 characters. Write it as an instruction you can read on the wrist mid-length: "KICK on back, no board, hips up". The app truncates longer labels and logs it.
  • kind is work or recovery. Rests are real rests.
  • Every violation is reported in one message, so the AI can fix them all at once.

A full example

The plan behind the example swim on the files page, plans/2026-05-13T19:02:11Z-s12.json in the repository's synthetic example folder: 1,200 m in six parts, about 36 minutes, as push_plan wrote it.

{
  "schema": "healthaccess.plan.v1",
  "id": "2026-05-13T19:02:11Z-s12",
  "name": "S12",
  "sport": "pool_swim",
  "pool_length_m": 50,
  "warmup": {"goal": {"type": "distance_m", "value": 200}, "zone": 1, "label": "FR easy, find your breathing"},
  "blocks": [
    {"repeat": 3, "steps": [
      {"kind": "work", "goal": {"type": "distance_m", "value": 50}, "zone": 1, "label": "DRILL 6-3-6: 6 kicks side, 3 strokes"},
      {"kind": "work", "goal": {"type": "distance_m", "value": 50}, "zone": 1, "label": "FR easy, long strokes"},
      {"kind": "recovery", "goal": {"type": "time_s", "value": 30}}
    ]},
    {"repeat": 4, "steps": [
      {"kind": "work", "goal": {"type": "distance_m", "value": 100}, "zone": 2, "label": "FR 100 steady, breathe every 3"},
      {"kind": "recovery", "goal": {"type": "time_s", "value": 40}}
    ]},
    {"repeat": 2, "steps": [
      {"kind": "work", "goal": {"type": "time_s", "value": 120}, "zone": 1, "label": "KICK on back, no board, hips up"},
      {"kind": "recovery", "goal": {"type": "time_s", "value": 45}}
    ]},
    {"repeat": 1, "steps": [
      {"kind": "work", "goal": {"type": "distance_m", "value": 100}, "zone": 2, "label": "FR 100, carry the drill into it"}
    ]}
  ],
  "cooldown": {"goal": {"type": "distance_m", "value": 100}, "zone": 1, "label": "Easy, any stroke, long exhale"},
  "notes": "Technique week 2. Short drill reps, then four steady 100s to carry the feel over. Kick on back is for the hips, not for speed. Target effort 5 to 6.",
  "created_by": "claude-code",
  "created_at": "2026-05-13T19:02:11Z",
  "scheduled_for": "2026-05-14T05:00:00Z"
}

The swim idea usually puts a two-minute time step first: the athlete starts the workout in the shower, so the Watch is already in swim mode at the pool edge and no length is lost to fumbling with a wet screen. This plan warms up over 200 m instead; both work.

What came back

The app's answer, plans/2026-05-13T19:02:11Z-s12.status.json, after WorkoutKit scheduled it for the next morning (method: schedule; a plan installed through Apple's sheet says preview and has no scheduled_for):

{
  "plan_id": "2026-05-13T19:02:11Z-s12",
  "state": "scheduled",
  "at": "2026-05-13T19:04:40Z",
  "method": "schedule",
  "workoutkit_id": "D4E7A2C1-6F3B-4E8A-9B5C-1A2F3E4D5C6B",
  "scheduled_for": "2026-05-14T05:00:00Z",
  "error": null
}

workoutkit_id is what links the finished swim back to this plan: the exported session on the files page carries plan_ref S12 and every step has its label back. get_plan_status returns the same fields; list_plans returns them for every plan in the folder, with planned distance and time.

Limits, all Apple's

  • Pool length cannot be set from a plan. WorkoutKit has no parameter for it; the Watch uses its own setting. The app shows pool_length_m as a reminder.
  • Step names on the Watch need iOS 18. On iOS 17 the workout installs without labels, and the app logs it.
  • Labels longer than 40 characters are cut.
  • Zone alerts an activity does not support are dropped and logged.
  • Sports: pool swim and indoor rowing today. Running and cycling plans are a question of adding goal types and testing, not of the format.
