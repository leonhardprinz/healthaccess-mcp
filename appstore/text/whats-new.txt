Cloud upload. Health Access can now upload your files to Google Drive, a WebDAV server (Nextcloud, a NAS) or S3 storage, straight from your iPhone.

Google Drive can't be picked as a folder on iPhone, so the app does the upload itself. It makes a new folder, Health Access, at the top level of your Drive. An AI that reads the drive can send workout plans back.

No synced folder? Choose "Keep it on this iPhone" in the first steps and switch on Cloud upload in Settings.

OneDrive and Box are planned. Tap one in Settings > Cloud upload to tell us you want it.
